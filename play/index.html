Controls

Physical US-QWERTY positions, whatever your layout prints. Keys are captured only while the screen has focus; clicking away clears anything held without pausing. Opposite directions resolve to the most recently pressed, and releasing it restores the one still held.

Saves
Battery SRAM is checked every five seconds while running, and flushed on pause, reset, navigation and when the tab is hidden. A crash or a forced browser termination can still lose the most recent few seconds. Two open tabs are not coordinated — the last write wins.
